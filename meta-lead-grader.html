Upload the Salesforce report of Added to Dialer / Attempted Contact / Open leads, then the Meta lead sheets. Each open lead is matched to its form answers and graded High / Med / Low on its likelihood to become an admit or a viable transfer.

What gets graded. Only the leads in the Salesforce report you upload — the ones still sitting in Added to Dialer, Attempted Contact or Open. The Meta sheets supply the form answers; nothing from them is graded unless it is also in the report.

Matching. If the Salesforce report includes a Phone column, leads match on the 10-digit number, which is near-exact. Without it, matching falls back to normalized first + last name (nicknames in parentheses dropped, punctuation removed), tie-broken by state and by the form lead created closest before the Salesforce create date. Adding Phone to the report is the single best way to raise the match rate.

What "success" means. A lead counts as a success if it appears in the Salesforce Admitted report (Meta lead sources), was transferred as a Viable VOB or admitted in the Transfer Status Report, or shows Transferred = Y and not non-viable in a facility lead tracker. Referrals and referred-out leads are not counted.

Training data. 29,228 Meta leads from 127 form tabs (Nov 2025 – Sep 2026). Transfer outcomes are only recorded from May 2026 on, so the scorecard is fit on the 13,651 leads created May 1 – Sep 3, 2026: 498 successes (3.65%), 84 of them admits.

Method. Every form phrases its questions a little differently, so answers are first mapped to shared buckets (who it's for, substance or struggle, duration, prior treatment, timeline, insurance yes/no, who provides it, carrier, barriers, withdrawal, policy-ID known, state, SUD vs MH form). A regularized logistic regression on those buckets produced the point values on the Scorecard tab (coefficient × 10, rounded). Leads with no insurance, Medicaid/Medicare or a state plan are pushed to Low by rule, since none of those admitted. Unanswered questions score 0.

Accuracy. On the training data the score ranks successes above non-successes 75% of the time (AUC 0.75); for admits alone, 67%. Useful for ordering a call list, not for declaring a lead dead. Partial grades (no form match) use only insurance and state and should be read as weaker.
